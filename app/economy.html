The Economy

Indonesia is the largest economy in Southeast Asia. Its nominal GDP was about US$1.40 trillion in 2024 (World Bank), or about US$4,925 per person, and it has grown by about 5% a year for most of the past decade.

GDP growth, 2015–2024

Real GDP growth, percent per year. The 2020 dip to −2.07% was the pandemic recession; growth bounced back to 5.31% in 2022 and has held near 5% since.

Inflation, 2015–2024

Consumer price inflation, annual average, percent. Inflation stayed inside Bank Indonesia's target band in most years; the 2022 spike to 5.51% followed the rise in subsidised fuel prices, and by 2024 it had eased to 1.57%.

Labour market

Unemployment was 4.86% in August 2024 (BPS) — the lowest in years — and the poverty rate was 8.57% in September 2024 (BPS), down from double digits a decade earlier.
