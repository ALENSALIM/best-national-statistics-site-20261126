Education

Almost every Indonesian child now goes to primary school, and adults are more literate than ever — about 96% of adults can read and write. The next challenge is keeping teenagers in school and getting more young people into higher education.

Years of schooling

The average Indonesian adult has completed about 9.1 years of schooling (BPS, 2024) — roughly the end of junior secondary school. A child starting school today can expect about 13.3 years of schooling (BPS, 2024), which would take them through senior secondary school and into the first years of higher education.
