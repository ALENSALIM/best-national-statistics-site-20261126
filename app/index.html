Indonesia in Numbers

The headline figures for the world's fourth most populous country — its people, its economy, its health and its schools — in one place. Every figure is a rounded published figure, shown with its year and source.

GDP growth, 2015–2024

Annual growth of real gross domestic product, percent per year. Indonesia grew every year in this decade except 2020, when the pandemic shrank the economy by 2.07%.

Source: Statistics Indonesia (BPS), 2015–2024. Figures rounded.

Population, 2015–2024

Indonesia's population rose from about 258 million in 2015 to about 283 million in 2024 — roughly 25 million more people in a decade.

Source: World Bank World Development Indicators, 2015–2024. Figures rounded to the nearest million.
