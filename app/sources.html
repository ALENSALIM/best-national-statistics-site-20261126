Sources & Methods

Every figure on NusaStats is a rounded published figure from one of two sources. No figure is estimated by this site, and no figure is loaded from anywhere else: the site works fully offline.

Sources

World Bank — World Development Indicators (WDI)

The World Bank's World Development Indicators, published at data.worldbank.org, supply the population series (2015–2024), GDP in current US dollars (2024), GDP per capita (2024), life expectancy (2015–2023), infant and under-five mortality (2015–2023), maternal mortality (2020), hospital beds and physicians, and tertiary enrolment (2024).

Statistics Indonesia — BPS

Statistics Indonesia (Badan Pusat Statistik), published at bps.go.id, supplies GDP growth (2015–2024), consumer price inflation (2015–2024), unemployment (August 2024), the poverty rate (September 2024), trade in goods (2024), sector shares of GDP (2024), the age structure (2024), province populations (2024 mid-year estimates), mean and expected years of schooling (2024), adult literacy (2024) and primary and secondary enrolment (2024).

Methods

  • Rounding. Population is rounded to the nearest million (provinces to one decimal place); growth and inflation to two decimals; life expectancy to one decimal; trade to the nearest billion US dollars; sector and age shares to whole percent. Rounding can make shares add to slightly more or less than 100%.
  • Years. Every figure carries its year next to it on the page. Where a page shows a "latest" figure for a year other than the one selected, it is labelled as the latest available.
  • Latest year. The latest year with a complete set of figures is 2024 for most series; life expectancy and mortality are latest in 2023, and maternal mortality in 2020.
  • No live data. All figures are fixed in the site's data file. The site makes no network request and fetches nothing from any other origin.

About this site

NusaStats is an independent, original statistics site built for the web. It is not affiliated with the World Bank or Statistics Indonesia, and it does not copy any other site's brand, text or layout. All design, text and charts are original. The site is static files only: it runs in any browser with no build step and no network connection.
