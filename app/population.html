Population

About 283.5 million people lived in Indonesia in 2024 (World Bank) — the fourth largest population in the world. Most live on Java: West, East and Central Java alone hold more than 128 million people.

Age structure, 2024

Indonesia has a young population: about a quarter of Indonesians are children under 15, and more than two in three are of working age. Only about 7% are 65 or older.
